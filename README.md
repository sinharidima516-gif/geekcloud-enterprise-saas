## 1. Context

GeekCloud is Mphasis's internal enterprise SaaS talent marketplace — it enables skill-based workforce allocation, workflow automation, and internal project staffing across business units. Think of it as an internal "gig marketplace" connecting contributors to internal work packages.

## 2. My Role

Supported business analysis and feature validation on the platform:
- Defined functional requirements, user stories, and acceptance criteria across the work-package lifecycle
- Partnered with product managers and engineering on feature scope and refinement

## 3. What I Did

- **UAT & functional validation** across 20 platform modules — onboarding, role-based access, skill management, marketplace discovery, approvals, reporting
- **User journey mapping** across 4 personas: Contributors, Work Package Owners, Delivery Heads, Platform Administrators
- **Agile support** — backlog refinement, defect triage, continuous enhancement cycles
- **Workflow/dashboard analysis** to surface adoption and utilization insights
- **Business rule validation** for contributor matching, approvals, task assignment, and incentive logic

## 4. Outcome

- 35% reduction in defects across the modules I validated, over ~39 sprints (~1.5 years)
